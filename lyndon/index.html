Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Lyndon

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and slows mineral buildup. Tankless owners should plan on descaling once a year if their water is hard.

How old is your water heater? The serial number on the label usually encodes the month and year it was built, and that date tells you a lot. Most tank heaters make it to 8 to 12 years, heat pump models 10 to 15, and well-maintained tankless units about 20. If your heater is beyond that point and is also showing rusty water, rumbling, or moisture at the base, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install after a leak.

If warning signs do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A dripping relief valve might point to excess pressure or a missing expansion tank, both inexpensive fixes. The same drip ignored for months can wear out the valve and stress the tank until the tank itself fails, and a small repair becomes a full replacement and a cleanup. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and ruined floors.

Hot Water Headaches Lyndon Homeowners Face

Whatever type of water heater your Lyndon home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tank heaters usually give trouble through thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric units lose heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
