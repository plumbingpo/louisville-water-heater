Kitchen Hot Water

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Few businesses work a water heater harder than a restaurant. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can outrun a small heater quickly, and the dish machine depends on a booster to reach its final rinse temperature. Louisville Water Heater services, sizes, and repairs the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we schedule service around your operating hours.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize at peak service. We verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and log every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

Your schedule comes first. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and downtime stays short so the kitchen opens on time.

Code & Inspections

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in tanks stored too cool, and expensive water damage from a ruptured tank. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Rapid Response

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

Every hour without hot water costs a business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the dish room. For a salon, it cancels appointments. For a hotel, it means refund requests, and in a multifamily building it leads to tenant disputes. Louisville Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties throughout the area, and we can schedule planned work after hours to keep downtime short.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit throwing error codes. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, restore hot water, and record every step for your service records. Then we put maintenance in place so the same failure doesn't return.

Preventive Care

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Louisville Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water hardness, usage patterns, and budget.

Compared with an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties that stay on a schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower fuel bills. Give us a call to talk through a plan for your property and budget.
